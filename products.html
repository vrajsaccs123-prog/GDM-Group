Product

Wheat, corn, chickpeas, pulses, gram flour and cattle feed. Current dal and besan rates come from the mill price sheet.

Wheat

Chakki fresh atta and the wheat range: wheat flour, maida, dalia and rawa. Wheat is milled for consistency and taste, for homes and for trade.

Corn

Corn products from the mill, supplied under our brands for food use and further processing.

Chickpeas

Kabuli, black and green chickpeas, cleaned and offered for wholesale.

Pulse

Chana dal, masoor, moong and matar, in bold, medium, dry and water grades, under Double Sher, Sunflower, Golden Queen, Green Diamond, Blue Diamond, Golden Coin, Rajwadi and Golden Hathoda.

Gram flour

Chana besan in super fine and mota, first and second quality, and matar besan (vatana flour), packed in 50 kg, 30 kg and 10 kg.

Cattle feed

Cattle feed with the protein, energy, minerals and vitamins required for growth. Chana churi is listed on the daily rate sheet.
