About Us

A dal and flour mill in Udyog Nagar, Indore, packing pulses and besan under its own brands.

Ghanshyam Dall Mill is a wheat milling company and manufacturer of agro products in India. Our primary products are wheat, durum wheat semolina, wheat fresh flour, healthy pulses, gram flour, cattle feed, maize, chickpeas, corn and many other products.

The mill works from Udyog Nagar, Navlakha, beside the Chhawani anaj mandi, and from the Nemawar Road plant in Dev Nagar. Traders buy chana dal, masoor, moong, matar, besan, daliya and chana churi under brands such as Double Sher, Golden Coin, Golden Queen, Double Hathoda, Sunflower, Green Diamond, Blue Diamond and Festival.

Goods are milled in the plant, kept untouched by hand where the process allows, and checked for quality, durability and consistency. The daily rate list on this website is the same sheet the mill updates.
